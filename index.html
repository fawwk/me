ProofLeads

My personal leads generation pipeline.

ProofLeads sends freelancers leads that come with proof. Each lead shows the business, evidence of its problem, who to contact and what to say first. A lead goes to three buyers at most, so nobody pitches into a crowd.

Code runs the checks and scores each lead. The language model only writes about facts already in the lead’s evidence file, and a lead without evidence never ships. A free feed of verified remote jobs and scam alerts brings people in.

aiogram and FastAPI bot, Neon Postgres, Paystack, GitHub Actions, Cloudflare Pages. Runs on free tiers.

Result computation and report cards for primary and secondary schools.
